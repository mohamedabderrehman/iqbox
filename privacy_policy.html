Information Collection and Use

For a better experience, while using our Service, we may collect certain personally identifiable information, as follows:

Account Information

We collect information you provide when you sign up, upgrade to a paid plan, or set up security features (such as your name, email address, payment info, and physical address).

Your Stuff

Our Services are designed to store your files, documents, photos, and videos ("Your Stuff"). To make this possible, we store and process Your Stuff and related info (file size, upload time, usage activity).

Usage Information

We collect info related to how you use the Services, including actions like sharing, editing, viewing, or moving files.

Device Information

We collect info from the devices you use to access IQBox, including IP addresses, browser types, device identifiers, and potentially location information depending on your settings.

Log Data

In case of an error in the app, we collect "Log Data" via third-party products. This includes your device IP address, operating system version, app configuration, and usage statistics.

Cookies

The IQBox website may use cookies to provide, improve, and protect our Services. You can choose to refuse these cookies in your browser settings, though some portions of the Service may not function correctly.

With Whom We Share Information

We do not sell your information to advertisers. We may share information only in the following cases:

We use third-party providers (support, IT services) to help us operate the Service. They access your info only to perform tasks on our behalf and are legally bound to keep it confidential.

We may disclose info if necessary to comply with the law, prevent fraud/abuse, protect someone from serious injury, or protect IQBox's legal rights.

Security

We strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting your Personal Information. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

Right to Have Data Deleted

You have the right to have your data deleted if it is no longer needed, if you oppose its use for marketing, or if it was used illegally.

Request Deletion

Please write to us at support@iqbox.site. We will review your request and delete the data within a reasonable timeframe.

Account Deletion

You can delete your IQBox account directly within the app. Most info is removed within a few days, though it may take up to 30 days to clear from backup systems.

Children's Privacy

IQBox does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal info from children under 13. If we discover a child under 13 has provided personal info, we will delete it immediately. If you are a parent and become aware of such a situation, please contact support@iqbox.site.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
